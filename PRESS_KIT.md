## 🎯 The Story: Why PDFchemy Matters in 2026

Modern document utilities have become hostile to users. Everyday tasks like reducing a PDF's file size for an email or rotating an upside-down page now come with:
* **Forced Cloud Uploads:** Exposing confidential medical, legal, and financial records to third-party web servers.
* **Aggressive Subscription Walls:** Demanding $10 to $30 every month just to download an un-watermarked document.
* **Artificial Paywall Quotas:** Limiting users to "2 free tasks per day" when they are under deadline.

**PDFchemy Tools was created as a direct counter-movement.** Built by an independent developer under a personal lifetime pledge, PDFchemy runs 100% offline on the user's processor. It is an emergency tool designed to be opened when a document needs to be fixed right now — without a paywall, account creation, or data leakage.

## ⚡ Core Feature Highlights for Reviewers

### 1. Smart Target-Size Compressor ("Fit Under 2MB")
* Rather than guessing with arbitrary percentage sliders, users select exact email or government upload limits (500 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB, 5 MB, or custom).
* An iterative binary search engine calculates the exact resolution and quantization curve on-device to guarantee the document fits under the target cap without unnecessary quality loss.

### 2. True Security Redaction Engine
* When redacting sensitive info (SSNs, tax IDs, credit cards), PDFchemy doesn't just draw a black rectangle overlay.
* It physically strips and erases the underlying text vector stream and raster pixels beneath the bounding box, making recovery via copy-pasting or reverse-engineering impossible.

### 3. Visual Page Studio
* High-speed thumbnail grid to rearrange, rotate (90° / 180°), duplicate, or delete individual pages.
* Single-click sequence reset and non-destructive output generation.

### 4. Full Conversion & E-Book Suite
* Images ⇄ PDF with direct bitstream pass-through.
* PDF ⇄ EPUB for Amazon Kindle, Kobo, and mobile e-readers.
* Comic Book Archives (`.cbz` / `.cbr`) ⇄ PDF.
* Multi-format data conversion: TXT, Markdown, HTML, CSV, JSON, YAML, and XML.

### 5. On-Device Vision AI (Camera Scanner & OCR)
* Live camera document capture with ML Kit edge detection, trapezoid deskewing, and shadow removal.
* Local OCR text extraction turning scanned physical paper into searchable, selectable PDFs.

## 🧪 Suggested Reviewer Test Workflows

To put PDFchemy through its paces during an editorial review or YouTube segment, we recommend testing these real-world stress scenarios:

1. **The Air-Gapped Test:** Put your device into **Airplane Mode** (Wi-Fi and mobile data OFF). Run a 50-page document compression, OCR text scan, or PDF-to-EPUB conversion. Every single feature executes natively at full speed.
2. **The Portal Fit Test:** Take a large, image-heavy 20MB scanned contract and use "Fit Under 2MB". Observe how the engine iteratively hits the exact size threshold.
3. **The Security Inspection:** Redact a paragraph, export the document, open it in any external PDF viewer, and attempt to highlight or copy-paste text from beneath the black box. The text is permanently purged.
4. **The Page Studio Rearrange:** Open a multi-page document, rotate landscape pages upright, drag-reorder pages, and delete blank annexes in under 15 seconds.
